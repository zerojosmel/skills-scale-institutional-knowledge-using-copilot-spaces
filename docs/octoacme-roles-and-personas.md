# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance & Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality strategies, ensure acceptance criteria compliance, and validate features before release. They own the testing plan and coordinate both manual and automated quality efforts.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Design and execute manual and automated test scenarios
- Validate acceptance criteria are met before feature acceptance
- Identify and triage quality issues with severity and priority
- Coordinate end-to-end smoke tests before releases
- Collaborate with developers on testability and CI/CD test configuration
- Track quality metrics and maintain test coverage dashboards

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal production defects
- Reduce time-to-resolution for critical issues
- Enable developers to ship with confidence
- Maintain clear quality metrics and dashboards
- Build a quality-first culture across the team

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA status in delivery syncs
- Test plan reviews during sprint planning
- Defect triage and acceptance sign-off in PR reviews
- Quality metrics reporting to Product Lead and Project Manager

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers:** Collaborate on test automation, testability improvements, and defect resolution
- **With Product Managers:** Align on acceptance criteria and quality expectations
- **With Project Managers:** Report quality status and risks; coordinate testing timelines
- **With Technical Leads:** Review test approach for complex features and technical debt

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approve scope and resources, and serve as the voice of business priorities. They enable decision-making and escalation pathways.

### Responsibilities
- Define business requirements and success criteria
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Make key trade-off decisions and prioritization calls
- Receive regular status updates and risk escalations
- Provide feedback and sign-off on deliverables
- Communicate outcomes to broader organization
- Resolve cross-functional dependencies and conflicts

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Reduce scope creep and unaligned expectations
- Enable quick resolution of cross-team dependencies
- Maximize ROI and stakeholder confidence
- Maintain strategic alignment with organizational goals

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder briefings
- Ad-hoc escalations via PM for decisions
- Release announcements and outcomes reporting
- Approval gates at key project milestones

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers:** Receive status updates and escalations; make decisions on scope and resources
- **With Product Managers:** Align on business objectives and success metrics
- **With Development Team:** Periodic demos and feedback sessions
- **With other Stakeholders:** Cross-functional coordination and dependency resolution

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define the technical direction, make architecture decisions, and ensure code quality and scalability. They guide the development team and resolve technical risks.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical architecture and technology choices
- Review and approve major technical decisions and PRs
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and debt
- Mentor developers and support capability building
- Ensure code quality, performance, and security standards
- Coordinate technical integration with dependent systems
- Define technical standards and best practices

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable systems
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable team capability and knowledge sharing
- Balance speed with long-term quality
- Drive continuous technical improvement

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Weekly technical risk reviews
- Architecture decision documentation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers:** Provide architecture guidance, code reviews, and technical mentorship
- **With QA/Testing Lead:** Review test approach for complex features; ensure testability
- **With Project Managers:** Flag technical risks and mitigation strategies
- **With Product Managers:** Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process blockers, and coach the team on continuous improvement. They focus on process health and team velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and process blockers
- Monitor team velocity and sprint health metrics
- Coach team on agile principles and practices
- Track and follow-up on retrospective action items
- Support process refinement and ceremony optimization
- Foster psychological safety and team collaboration

### Goals
- Maximize team throughput and predictability
- Build a psychologically safe, high-performing team
- Continuously improve processes based on team feedback
- Reduce cycle time and unplanned work
- Maintain team morale and engagement

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation
- Sprint ceremony leadership
- Retrospective notes and action item tracking
- Velocity and process metrics reporting

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers:** Coordinate on scheduling and escalation of blockers
- **With Developers:** Coach on agile practices and remove impediments
- **With all team members:** Facilitate collaboration and psychological safety

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Reference these definitions when assigning roles in project charters and team compositions.
- Use the "Interactions with Other Roles" sections to understand cross-functional dependencies and communication patterns.
